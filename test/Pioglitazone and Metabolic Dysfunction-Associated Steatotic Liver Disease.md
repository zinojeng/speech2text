# 幻燈片內容分析

## 標題
**Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease (MASLD)**

## 主要內容
- **最常見的慢性肝病原因之一**
- **20%-30%的MAFLD患者會發展為MASH**
- **增加肝硬化及肝細胞癌的風險**
- **增加心血管疾病（CV）、CV及肝相關死亡率的風險**

## 視覺元素
- **圖示**：
  - 右側顯示了相關的病理狀況和其關聯：
    - **Obesity** （肥胖）
    - **NAFLD** (非酒精性脂肪肝疾病)
    - **NASH** (非酒精性脂肪性肝炎)
    - **Cirrhosis** (肝硬化)
    - **T2DM** (2型糖尿病)
  
  - 相關的醫學狀況：
    - **Hypogonadism** (性腺功能低下)
    - **PCOS** (多囊卵巢綜合症)
    - **Hypothyroidism** (甲狀腺功能減退)
    - **GH Deficiency** (生長激素缺乏)

## 引用
Belfort-DeAguiar et al. JCEM, 2023

這些內容提供了有關MASLD的資訊，包括其與其他健康問題的關係以及相關風險。

- **1962**: Recognized as a disease
- **1980**: Named **Nonalcoholic steatohepatitis (NASH)**, associated with obesity, diabetes, and dyslipidemia

## 標題
**MASLD Consensus from ADA - 2025**

## 中心圖形
- **MASLD**
  - 包含不同階段的MASLD（代謝性肝臟疾病）。

### 圖片分區
1. **Individualize care, targeting the following:**
   - Adoption of a healthy lifestyle
   - Weight loss (if indicated)
   - Optimal diabetes management
   - Cardiovascular risk reduction
   - Need for metabolic surgery (as recommended by guidelines)

2. **MASLD 分類**
   - MASLD with F0-F1
   - MASLD with F2-F3 ("at-risk" MASH)
   - Compensated cirrhosis
   - Decompensated cirrhosis

## 右側內容
### Obesity pharmacotherapy
- Prefer GLP-1RA, dual GIP/GLP-1RA for:
  - MASLD with F0-F1
  - MASLD with F2-F3
  - Consider caution for MASLD with F2-F3.

### Diabetes pharmacotherapy
- Prefer GLP-1RA, dual GIP/GLP-1RA, pioglitazone, SGLT2i for:
  - MASLD with F0-F1
  - MASLD with F2-F3
  - As with F2-F3 with caution for compensated cirrhosis.
  
### MASH pharmacotherapy
- Not indicated for MASLD with F0-F1.
- Resmetirom, GLP-1RA for MASLD with F2-F3.
- Only use insulin for decompensated cirrhosis.

## 注意事項
- **AVOID** in decompensated cirrhosis.
