# Tackling MASLD Burden of Disease Through Its Entire Spectrum

## Pyramid Structure

### 1. Cirrhosis
- **Population (N)**: 1 million

### 2. NASH (MASH)
- **Description**: Advanced fibrosis (F3/F4)
- **Criteria**: 
  - FIB-4 > 2.6 
  - LSM 15-25 
  - Normal platelets
- **Population (N)**: 2 million

### 3. At Risk NASH (MASH)
- **Description**: 
  - NASH + high activity + >= F2 fibrosis
- **Criteria**:
  - FIB-4 (1.3-2.6)
  - Liver stiffness > 10<20 
  - Normal platelets
- **Population (N)**: 6-8 million

### 4. MASLD
- **Description**: 
  - Fatty liver, steatohepatitis, fibrosis stages 0-1
- **Criteria**: 
  - FIB-4 < 1.3
- **Population (N)**: Approx. 60 million

### Main Cause of Death
- **Reasons**: Cardiometaobolic, cancers

## Question
- **Text**: "Will a wipe out of SLD avoid progression?"

### Notes
- **SLD**: Steatotic Liver Disease
- **Source**: Adapted from Estes et al., Hepatology 2018

## Key Takeaways

- **GLP-1 receptor agonists** are potent agents that promote weight loss and can also improve both steatohepatitis and fibrosis, although they have not yet been shown to be beneficial in cirrhosis.
  
- **GLP-1 anchored dual and triple agonists** are promising classes of molecules with encouraging data from phase 2 trials.
  
- While **weight-loss independent effects** may exist, a substantial portion of GLP-1 benefits in MASH are related to weight loss.
  
- **Weight loss** improves insulin resistance and systemic metabo-inflammatory cues driving MASH via complex effects that may involve its effects in the CNS, gut, adipose tissue, and immune-inflammatory system.
